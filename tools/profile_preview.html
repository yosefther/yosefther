Iris and Lens Profile

Iris and lens, round three

I rendered round two, looked at it, and fixed what was weak. The biggest change is the header: the machine half is no longer an eye with a steel plate behind it. It is a round camera lens that shares its centre with the human iris.

  • Half eye, half lensThe left side keeps the almond eye, lashes and skin. The right side is a full lens barrel with a grip ring, an engraved ring (YA-26 · 35mm 1:2.8), a focus scale and coated glass.
  • One signal across the headerThe heartbeat runs along the bottom, hits the seam, and continues as data bits. Before, it stopped at a box and went nowhere.
  • Nothing collidesLabels, traces and readouts no longer overlap. Small steel staples now hold the two halves together at the seam.
  • Calmer cardsThe heartbeat divider is kept for section panels only. Cards are shorter with no dead space, and the rayCast3D art is a real ray-cast that shades from skin tone to cyan.
